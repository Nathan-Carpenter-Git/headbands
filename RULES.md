# How to Play Headbands

Headbands is a guessing game for two or more players.
Everyone can see the card on your forehead except you.
Figure out what your own card says before everyone else figures out theirs.

## What you need

- Two or more players, each on their own phone or computer.
- A voice call so you can talk to each other, such as Discord or FaceTime.
  The game shows the cards, but the questions and answers happen out loud.

## Setting up

1. One player creates a lobby and becomes the party leader.
2. The leader shares the invite link, and everyone else joins with it.
3. The leader picks one or more categories, how many rounds to play, and a couple of options.
4. The leader starts the game.

## Playing a round

1. Every player is dealt a card from the chosen category.
2. You can see everyone else's card, but your own card is hidden from you.
3. Take turns asking the group yes or no questions about your card, out loud.
   Examples: "Am I an animal?" or "Would I find this in a kitchen?"
4. Everyone else answers honestly, since they can all see your card.
5. When you think you know what your card is, say your guess out loud and press the reveal button.

Don't like your card?
You can swap it for a different one as many times as you want before you reveal.
Swapping is always free, so nobody can read anything into whether you do it.

## Scoring

Your score depends on how early you reveal your card.
The first player to reveal gets the most points, and each later player gets one point fewer.
The last player to reveal gets nothing.

For example, in a five player round:

| Place | Points |
| ----- | ------ |
| 1st   | 5      |
| 2nd   | 4      |
| 3rd   | 3      |
| 4th   | 2      |
| 5th   | 0      |

Points add up across rounds.
After each round you'll see the standings, and then the leader starts the next one.
When the last round ends, the player with the most points wins.

## Options the leader can choose

- **Categories:** pick one for the whole game, or pick several and the game moves through them round by round.
- **Randomize order:** shuffle the order the categories come up in.
- **Rounds:** how many rounds to play.
- **Auto reveal the last player:** once everyone else has revealed, the last player's card is revealed for them and the round ends.
  Turn this off if you want the last player to keep guessing.

## Good to know

- If a category has fewer cards than players, a few cards will repeat.
  Duplicates are kept to the minimum needed.
- You can build your own categories from the category library and share them with friends as files.
- If your phone locks or your connection drops, come back within two minutes and you'll pick up right where you left off.
