# How to Play Headbands

Headbands has two game modes, and the party leader picks one in the lobby before starting.
**Headbands** is the classic forehead guessing game, and **Spybands** is a hidden spy game played with the same categories.
The rules for Spybands are [further down](#spybands).

## Headbands

Headbands is a guessing game for two or more players.
Everyone can see the card on your forehead except you.
Figure out what your own card says before everyone else figures out theirs.

### What you need

- Two or more players, each on their own phone or computer.
- A voice call so you can talk to each other, such as Discord or FaceTime.
  The game shows the cards, but the questions and answers happen out loud.

### Setting up

1. One player creates a lobby and becomes the party leader.
2. The leader shares the invite link, and everyone else joins with it.
3. The leader picks one or more categories, how many rounds to play, and a couple of options.
   Everyone else can see these settings in the lobby, but only the leader can change them.
4. The leader starts the game.

### Playing a round

1. Every player is dealt a card from the chosen category.
2. You can see everyone else's card, but your own card is hidden from you.
3. Take turns asking the group yes or no questions about your card, out loud.
   Examples: "Am I an animal?" or "Would I find this in a kitchen?"
4. Everyone else answers honestly, since they can all see your card.
5. When you think you know what your card is, say your guess out loud (only on your turn).
6. If you are correct, press the reveal button!

Don't like your card?
You can swap it for a different one as many times as you want before you reveal.
Swapping is always free, so nobody can read anything into whether you do it.

### Scoring

Your score depends on how early you reveal your card.
The first player to reveal gets the most points, and each later player gets one point fewer.
The last player to reveal gets nothing.

For example, in a five player round:

| Place | Points |
| ----- | ------ |
| 1st   | 5      |
| 2nd   | 4      |
| 3rd   | 3      |
| 4th   | 2      |
| 5th   | 0      |

Points add up across rounds.
After each round you'll see the standings, and then the leader starts the next one.
When the last round ends, the player with the most points wins.

### Options the leader can choose

- **Categories:** pick one for the whole game, or pick several and the game moves through them round by round.
- **Randomize order:** shuffle the order the categories come up in.
- **Rounds:** how many rounds to play.

## Spybands

Spybands is a social deduction game for three or more players, inspired by Spyfall.
Everyone shares the same secret card, except one randomly chosen spy who doesn't see it.
The players try to find the spy, and the spy tries to blend in without getting caught.

### Setting up

Setup works the same as Headbands.
The leader picks Spybands as the game mode, chooses categories and how many rounds to play, and starts the game once at least three players have joined.

### Agreeing on the card

1. A random card is drawn from the category, and everyone except the spy can see it.
   The spy only sees that they are the spy, plus a shuffled list of 20 cards from the category with the real one hidden among them.
   If the category has fewer than 20 cards, the spy sees all of them.
2. Anyone can vote to swap the card for a new one.
   Once more than half of the players vote to swap, a new card is drawn, a new spy is chosen at random, and everyone has to look again.
   The new spy is always someone who wasn't the spy before, so nobody carries the role across a card change.
3. When you're happy with the card, press Ready.
   The round starts once every player is ready.

The spy takes part in all of this too, so they can blend in by voting and readying up like everyone else.

### Finding the spy

1. Talk it out over voice chat.
   Ask each other anything about the card, it doesn't have to be a yes or no question.
   Answer in a way that proves you know the card, without making it so obvious that the spy can work it out.
2. At any point, vote for the player you think is the spy.
   You can change or take back your vote whenever you like.
3. Once more than half of all players vote for the same person, a ten second countdown starts.
   If enough people change their vote to break that majority, the countdown stops.
4. When the countdown reaches zero, the vote is locked in.
   Everyone's role is revealed, and the spy finally sees the card.

### The spy's guess

Instead of waiting for the vote, the spy can try to guess the card, as long as no vote is counting down yet.

1. The spy says their guess out loud, then taps **Guess the card**.
   Voting pauses for everyone.
2. The card appears on the spy's screen, and they have five seconds to tap **I got it** if it matches what they said, or **I missed it** if it doesn't.
   If time runs out, it counts as a miss.
3. The spy only gets one guess per round, and the round ends either way.

This runs on trust: nobody checks the spy's answer, so be honest.

### Scoring

| Result                    | Points                          |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| The spy is voted out      | Every other player gets 1 point |
| Someone else is voted out | The spy gets 2 points           |
| The spy guesses the card  | The spy gets 4 points           |
| The spy guesses wrong     | Every other player gets 1 point |

Points add up across rounds, just like in Headbands.

If the spy leaves the game for good during a round, or so many players leave that fewer than three remain, the round is called off and nobody scores.

## Good to know

- You can build your own categories from the category library and share them with friends as files.
- If your phone locks or your connection drops, come back within two minutes and you'll pick up right where you left off.
- If someone leaves for good, the leader can remove them from the lobby with the Kick button, so the game isn't held up waiting for them.
